Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information Growth Guru Agency (“GGA”, “we”) collects through this website and what we do with it. The short version: we only use your details to reply to you and work with you, and we never sell them.

What we collect

  • Contact form. When you send us a message, we receive what you type: your name, email, company, website or social handle, the service you're interested in and your message.
  • Strategy call bookings. If you book a call, you enter your name, email and any answers you choose to give into our booking calendar.
  • Emails. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include.
  • Basic visit statistics. Our hosting provider may count page visits in an anonymous, cookie-free way so we can see which pages are useful.

Services we use

  • Formspree delivers contact form messages to our inbox. Your message passes through Formspree's servers on the way to us. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Calendly runs our booking calendar. The booking window loads only when you click a booking button. See Calendly's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and keeps it fast and secure. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your message or prepare for your strategy call.
  • To provide our services if you become a client.
  • To keep a simple record of our conversations with you.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you open the booking calendar, Calendly may set its own cookies to make the booking work.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Just email us and we'll take care of it.
