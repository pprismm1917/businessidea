FAQ

Straight answers.

Don't see your question? Ask us — a real person will answer.

Do I own my website & domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and your content — photos, text, logo — is always yours. Want to take the design with you someday? A design buyout is available.

What if I already have a website?

No problem. We'll look at what you have, keep what's working, and build a faster, phone-friendly version. We'll handle moving your domain over when you're ready to switch.

How long does it take?

Your free draft is usually ready in about 48 hours. Once you approve it, your site is live in about 7 days.

Is there a contract?

The monthly plan has a 12-month minimum, and you'll see every term in writing before you sign. You don't pay anything until you've approved your design. Prefer to pay once? Ask us about a one-time build.

Do you only work with fence companies?

Fence & deck companies in DFW — that's all we do. That focus means your site is built around how homeowners actually shop for a fence or deck.

Where are you based?

We're a remote team and work Texas business hours.

What do you need from me?

Your company name, phone number, service area and a few project photos. That's it. No photos handy? We can pull them from your Google or Facebook page.
