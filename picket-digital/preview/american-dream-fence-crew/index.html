Fence repair

Fixed, when a repair will do the job

Storms, time, and everyday wear take a toll on even the best-built fence. We offer fast, affordable fence repair for wood, chain link, and wrought iron fences and gates, so you don’t have to pay for a full replacement when a repair will do the job.

  1. 01

    Post & panel repair

    We reset posts in concrete, replace rotted pickets and rails, and match materials so the repair blends in.

  2. 02

    Gate repair & realignment

    We repair and realign hinges, latches, and frames, and rebuild gates that have shifted out of square.

  3. 03

    Wind & storm damage repair

    North Texas storms can flatten sections of fence overnight. Call 469-369-7949 for a free estimate.

About

Founded in 2006 by owner and operator Bobby Quaid

With over 20 years of experience in fencing, carpentry, stage building and home construction, Bobby founded American Dream Builders, the parent company of American Dream Fence Crew. Residential, commercial, custom and builder work, with complete custom design services, estimates and consulting.

We use only the highest quality materials and adhere to industry standard best practices.

“Our mission is to actively serve our customers while giving back to our community every chance we get. We can handle any project. No project is too small, no project is too big. We handle projects all the way from residential, commercial, to custom application and builders.”
