## Services (their menu, exactly these five)
1. **Custom Wood Fences** — cedar. Styles in their portfolio: 6 ft and 8 ft **board-on-board**, 6 ft and 8 ft **side-by-side**, 8 ft **double-face**, with cap rail/trim; walk gates with decorative hardware.
2. **Decks** — cedar decks, pool decks, covered decks/pergola-style covers appear in photos. About page also mentions **Patio Covers**.
3. **Ornamental Iron Fences** — black ornamental iron, pool enclosures, front-yard iron.
4. **Automatic Gates** — swing gates (wood and iron), decorative iron driveway gates (one with a star). A 17 ft gate with a solar motor is described in Richard's testimonial, but the full review says a *different* company installed the solar motor and nothing on their site lists solar gates, so do NOT claim solar-powered gates as a Blackwell service.
5. **Chain Link Fence** — black vinyl-coated and galvanized chain link in photos.

## How they build (their FAQ page, verbatim facts)
- Posts set **3 ft deep on 5 ft 9 in centers, in concrete**.
- **2 3/8 in, .095 gauge galvanized steel posts**.
- Pickets: **#1 Western Red cedar** or **clear cedar** ("A clear picket doesn't have the knots a traditional #1 cedar picket has").
- Nails: **Duo-Fast aluminum ring shank nails**.
- Rails: **2x4 rough cedar rails, appearance grade**.
- Staining: they **recommend staining 4–6 weeks after installation**, not a paint-based stain. **Blackwell Fence does not stain fences.**
- They do **not** quote pre-dipped wood ("After twenty plus years experience, I have yet to see a good pre-dipped product").
