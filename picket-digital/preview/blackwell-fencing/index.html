Our standard

How we build a fence that lasts

A fence is only as good as what’s under the ground and behind the pickets. This is how every Blackwell cedar fence goes up.

Spec sheet Cedar privacy fence · Blackwell Fencing
  1. 01

    Posts

    2 3/8 in., .095 gauge galvanized steel. Steel, not wood.

  2. 02

    Set

    3 ft deep, in concrete, on 5 ft 9 in centers.

  3. 03

    Pickets

    #1 Western Red cedar, or clear cedar if you want it without the knots.

  4. 04

    Rails

    2x4 rough cedar, appearance grade.

  5. 05

    Nails

    Duo-Fast aluminum ring-shank nails.

  6. 06

    Stain

    We recommend staining 4–6 weeks after installation, and not with a paint-based stain. Blackwell doesn’t stain fences, and we don’t quote pre-dipped wood.

Questions

Straight answers

How deep do you set the posts?

3 feet deep, in concrete, on 5 ft 9 in centers.

What kind of posts do you use?

2 3/8 in., .095 gauge galvanized steel posts.

What is the difference between #1 cedar and clear cedar pickets?

A clear picket doesn’t have the knots a traditional #1 cedar picket has. We build with #1 Western Red cedar or clear cedar, your choice.

What are the rails made of?

2x4 rough cedar rails, appearance grade.

What nails do you use?

Duo-Fast aluminum ring shank nails.

Should I stain my new fence? Do you stain?

We recommend staining 4 to 6 weeks after installation, and not with a paint-based stain. Blackwell Fence does not stain fences.

Do you offer pre-dipped (pre-stained) wood?

No. After twenty plus years of experience, we have yet to see a good pre-dipped product, so we don’t quote it.
